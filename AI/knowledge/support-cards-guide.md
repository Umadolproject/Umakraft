## What Are Support Cards?

Support cards are the backbone of Career Mode. You bring a deck of **6 cards** (5 of yours + 1 friend's) into each run. They determine:
- Training stat bonuses
- Events that trigger during training
- Skills your trainee can learn
- Energy recovery and mood boosts

A smart deck can make ANY Uma Musume a champion.

## How Support Cards Work During Training

Each turn, your support cards are randomly assigned to the 5 training courses. When you pick a course:
- Cards assigned to that course give **stat bonuses** proportional to their level
- **Friendship gauge** builds over time — max friendship unlocks bigger bonuses
- Cards trigger **events** that give stats, skills, energy, or mood

## Deck Building Principles

### 1. Balance your colors
A typical balanced deck: **2 Speed + 2 Stamina + 1 Power + 1 Wit**
- Adjust based on your build goal — Sprint might go 3 Speed / 1 Stamina / 1 Power / 1 Wit

### 2. Level your cards
SSR cards at level 30+ unlock their full potential. Level 40+ is ideal. SR cards at max level (40) can outperform low-level SSRs.

### 3. Match to your trainee's strategy
- **Front Runners**: Speed-heavy deck (3 Speed + 2 Stamina + 1 Power)
- **Pace Chasers**: Balanced (2 Speed + 2 Stamina + 1 Power + 1 Wit)
- **Late Surge / End Closer**: Stamina + Power heavy

### 4. Scenario-linked cards
Some cards have special bonuses in specific scenarios. Check the card description!

## Must-Have Cards (EN)

### SSR Super Creek (Piece of Mind) — Stamina
- Iconic recovery skill: **Swinging Maestro** (recovers endurance on corners, ANY track)
- Extremely long meta lifespan (~2 years in JP)
- Priority #1 to save for

### SSR Fine Motion (Wave of Gratitude) — Wit
- Wit cards are used in EVERY scenario and EVERY track type
- Universal investment — always useful

### SSR Kitasan Black (Fire at My Heels) — Speed
- The most iconic Speed card in the game
- Built for Front Runners but used universally
- Releases with the 5th set of scouts — save Carats!

### SSR Rice Shower (Happiness Just around the Bend)
- Powerful choice for the Aoharu Hai (Unity Cup) scenario
- Strong meta pick

## Quick Tips

1. **SSR > SR > R** — but a max-level SR beats a level-1 SSR
2. **Friend slot matters** — borrow the strongest card you don't own
3. **Level support cards FIRST** — they determine your training quality more than which Uma you pick
4. **Super Creek is the #1 save target** at launch — her card stays meta for years
5. **SSR Haru Urara (Guts) is FREE** — available in the Friend Point shop!
6. **Speed + Stamina** is the training foundation — everything else builds on it

## Card Events

Support card events trigger randomly during training turns. They can:
- Give bonus stats (+10–20 to one or more stats)
- Grant skill hints (learn skills at reduced SP cost)
- Restore energy
- Boost mood
- Give rare skills

**Pro tip**: Multi-stage events (indicated by arrows) can lead to powerful rare skills — choose dialogue options that continue the chain!
