## A

### Air Groove
- **Alias**: The Empress (女帝)
- **Personality**: Cool, strict senpai who expects excellence. Has a soft spot for hard workers. Keeps Gold Ship in line.
- **Racing Style**: All-rounder — middle-distance turf. Strong late kick with excellent stamina.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1997 Tenno Sho (Autumn) and 1997 Japan Cup Dirt. Known as the "Empress".
- **VA**: Aoki Ruriko
- **Relationships**: admired by Daiwa Scarlet; feared by everyone

---

## B

### Biwa Hayahide
- **Personality**: Quiet, stoic beauty with an almost ethereal presence. Rarely shows emotion but deeply cares about her rivals — especially Narita Brian.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance specialist (3000m+). Incredible stamina and pacing.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1994 Kikuka Sho and 1994 Tenno Sho (Spring). Nicknamed "The Monster of the Heisei Era".
- **VA**: Kondo Yui

---

## D

### Daiwa Scarlet
- **Alias**: Miss Perfect — "Second-best won't cut it!"
- **Personality**: Proud, competitive, and cheeky. Plays the perfect honor student but hates losing more than anything. Writes fan letters to manga authors. Can draw straight lines without a ruler.
- **Racing Style**: Front-running middle-distance specialist. Devastating pace at 1600m–2400m. Never placed below 2nd in her real-horse career.
- **Real Horse**: Won 2007 Arima Kinen (as a 3yo filly!), 2008 Tenno Sho (Autumn). Lost the legendary 2cm photo finish to Vodka in the 2008 Tenno Sho (Autumn). Sired by Agnes Tachyon.
- **Iconic Rivalry**: Vodka — roommates and rivals. Their 2007–2008 battles (including the 2cm photo finish) defined an era.
- **VA**: Chisa Kimura
- **Height**: 163cm (2cm shorter than Vodka — because Vodka beat her by 2cm!)

### Dream Journey
- **Personality**: Mysterious strategist who sees racing as chess. Plays chess with Daiwa Scarlet.
- **VA**: —

## E

### El Condor Pasa
- **Personality**: Passionate, theatrical performer. Treats racing like art. Aims to be "the world's strongest Umamusume".
- **Racing Style**: Powerful middle-distance runner who adapts to any pace.
- **Real Horse**: First Japanese-trained horse to win a G1 in France (1999 Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud). 2nd in 1999 Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe.
- **VA**: Takahashi Minami
- **Relationship**: Member of the "Golden Generation" with Special Week, Seiun Sky, King Halo, Grass Wonder

---

## F

### Fuji Kiseki
- **Personality**: Elegant, princely figure admired by all. Charismatic and effortlessly cool.
- **Racing Style**: Short-distance speed demon. Explosive acceleration over 1200m–1600m.
- **Real Horse**: Undefeated in 4 starts (1992–1995). Won 1995 Asahi Hai Futurity Stakes. Legendary sire.
- **VA**: Matsui Eriko

---

## G

### Gold Ship
- **Alias**: Goru-chan (ゴルシちゃん)
- **Personality**: Absolute agent of chaos. Unpredictable, mischievous, loves causing trouble — especially for trainer-kun. Surprisingly insightful and loyal.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance specialist with terrifying late-race acceleration.
- **Real Horse**: Won 6 G1 races. Known for unpredictable behavior — sometimes refused to enter the gate. Fan favorite.
- **VA**: Uesaka Sumire (originally announced as Daiwa Scarlet's VA!)
- **Teammate**: Team Spica (anime)

### Grass Wonder
- **Personality**: Mysterious, cool beauty with an American-flavored speech style. Gives wise advice to Special Week. Keeps others at a distance but fiercely competitive.
- **Racing Style**: Middle-distance turf specialist with incredible turn of foot.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1998 Asahi Hai Futurity and 1999 Arima Kinen. One of two Golden Generation horses Special Week never defeated.
- **VA**: Maeda Kaori
- **Trivia**: After retirement, was placed next to Special Week at the farm — but Spe didn't like him much!

---

## H

### Haru Urara
- **Alias**: The People's Horse (みんなのウマ娘)
- **Personality**: Cheerful, eternally optimistic underdog who never gives up. Zero wins but infinite spirit. Helped Special Week find her way around Tracen on day one.
- **Racing Style**: Tries her absolute best!
- **Real Horse**: Lost ALL 113 starts. Became a national sensation as a symbol of perseverance. Cultural icon.
- **VA**: Iida Hikaru

## I

### Ines Fujin
- **Personality**: Stoic, traditional warrior with samurai-like honor.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance specialist. Grinds opponents down over 2400m+.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1990 Kikuka Sho and 1991 Tenno Sho (Spring).
- **VA**: Hashimoto Chinami

---

## K

### King Halo
- **Personality**: Proud, dramatic speedster who believes he's destined for greatness. Gets inspiration from Special Week's dream of being Japan's best.
- **Racing Style**: Short-to-middle-distance front-runner (1200m–1600m).
- **Real Horse**: Won 2000 Takamatsunomiya Kinen (G1 sprint). Sired many top sprinters.
- **VA**: Sakura Ayane
- **Trivia**: Member of the Golden Generation

### Kitasan Black
- **Personality**: Cheerful, earnest, and endlessly determined. Loves festivals and celebrations. Idolized Tokai Teio.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance powerhouse. Dominated the 2016–2017 JRA seasons.
- **Real Horse**: Won 7 G1 races including 2016 Japan Cup and 2017 Tenno Sho (Spring + Autumn). Horse of the Year 2016 and 2017.
- **VA**: Yano Hinaki
- **Teammate**: Team Spica (anime Season 3)

---

## M

### Manhattan Cafe
- **Personality**: Mysterious, quiet, and drawn to the "depths" of racing. Has an almost supernatural aura.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance specialist with devastating stamina.
- **Real Horse**: Won 2001 Kikuka Sho and 2002 Tenno Sho (Spring).
- **VA**: Koshimizu Ami

### Maruzensky
- **Alias**: The Legendary Speedster
- **Personality**: Cool, mature onee-san with a playful teasing side. Loves fast cars and faster races. A mentor figure.
- **Racing Style**: Pure speed. Undefeated in Japan before the G1 era existed.
- **Real Horse**: Undefeated in 8 starts in Japan. Nicknamed "The Horse Who Was Too Fast" — G1 races didn't exist during his prime.
- **VA**: Lynn

### Meisho Doto
- **Personality**: Gentle giant with a heart of gold. Often overshadowed by T.M. Opera O but quietly works harder than anyone.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance grinder through sheer persistence.
- **Real Horse**: Finished 2nd to T.M. Opera O SIX times. Finally beat him in the 2001 Takarazuka Kinen. The ultimate "always the bridesmaid" story.
- **VA**: Misawa Sachika

### Mejiro McQueen
- **Alias**: The Long-Distance Emperor
- **Personality**: Refined, aristocratic ojou-sama from the Mejiro family. Graceful but fiercely competitive. Best friends/rivals with Tokai Teio.
- **Racing Style**: Dominant long-distance stayer (2400m+). Dares anyone to keep up.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1991 & 1992 Tenno Sho (Spring) back-to-back. Nicknamed "The Long-Distance Emperor".
- **VA**: Onishi Saori
- **Teammate**: Team Spica (anime)

### Mihono Bourbon
- **Personality**: Emotionless cyborg-like runner who treats racing as precise calculations. Actually has a pure, sincere heart — shows it through actions.
- **Racing Style**: Front-running specialist. Machine-like metronomic pace.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1992 Satsuki Sho and Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby). Known for relentless front-running.
- **VA**: Hasegawa Ikumi

---

## N

### Narita Brian
- **Alias**: The Shadowy Monster (影の怪物)
- **Personality**: Dark, brooding lone wolf. Keeps everyone at arm's length. Possesses terrifying natural talent. Respects Biwa Hayahide.
- **Racing Style**: Devastating all-rounder. Known for appearing from nowhere in the stretch to destroy the field.
- **Real Horse**: Won the 1994 Triple Crown by a combined margin of 16+ lengths. His 7-length Kikuka Sho win is legendary.
- **VA**: Aisaka Yuuka

### Nice Nature
- **Personality**: Laid-back, easygoing, and famously "almost good enough." Always places but rarely wins. Beloved for her relatable mediocrity.
- **Racing Style**: Consistent placer — always in the money, rarely first.
- **Real Horse**: Finished 3rd more times than almost any G1 horse. A fan favorite for always being "right there."
- **VA**: Maeda Kaori

---

## O

### Oguri Cap
- **Alias**: The Grey Idol (芦毛の怪物)
- **Personality**: Pure-hearted country boy who LOVES food. Innocent and hardworking. Beloved by everyone, especially his hometown of Kasamatsu.
- **Racing Style**: Powerful all-rounder. Surge acceleration that overwhelms any opponent.
- **Real Horse**: Won 12 G1 races. His come-from-behind victory in the 1990 Arima Kinen (retirement race) is one of the greatest moments in Japanese racing.
- **VA**: Takayanagi Tomoyo
- **Iconic Rivalry**: Tamamo Cross — the "Grey Derby" of 1988.

---

## R

### Rice Shower
- **Alias**: The Giant Killer
- **Personality**: Quiet, shy, and self-conscious. Has an enormous appetite rivaling Oguri Cap. Struggles with self-doubt but finds incredible strength when it matters.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance specialist with devastating closing speed.
- **Real Horse**: Stopped Mejiro McQueen's Triple Crown bid (1992 Kikuka Sho) AND Mihono Bourbon's Triple Crown bid (1993 Kikuka Sho). The ultimate spoiler.
- **VA**: Iwami Manaka

---

## S

### Sakura Bakushin O
- **Personality**: Energetic school idol type. Motto: "Bakushin!" (full speed ahead!).
- **Racing Style**: Pure short-distance sprinter (1200m). Explosive gate speed — nobody catches her over 1200m.
- **Real Horse**: Japan's greatest sprinter. Won Sprinters Stakes (G1) twice.
- **VA**: Mimori Suzuko

### Seiun Sky
- **Personality**: Laid-back, sleepy trickster. Looks like he just rolled out of bed but is secretly a genius tactician.
- **Racing Style**: Escapist front-runner who lulls the field to sleep with a slow early pace, then bolts away.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1998 Satsuki Sho and Kikuka Sho. Famous for "steal" victories.
- **VA**: Kitou Akari
- **Trivia**: Member of the Golden Generation with Special Week

### Silence Suzuka
- **Alias**: The Silent Streak (異次元の逃亡者)
- **Personality**: Ethereal, otherworldly beauty who exists only to run. Almost emotionless — lives entirely in the race. Special Week's idol and roommate.
- **Racing Style**: Perfect front-runner (大逃げ) — maximum speed from the gate, runs in his own dimension. 2000m–2400m turf.
- **Real Horse**: Undefeated in 1998 until a tragic breakdown in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). Became a legend. His half-sibling Special Week won the same race one year later.
- **VA**: Kouno Marika
- **Teammate**: Team Spica (anime), Team Sirius (game)

### Smart Falcon
- **Personality**: Cool, sharp-tongued beauty who's secretly a massive dork for her trainer. Obsessed with being "smart" and efficient.
- **Racing Style**: Dirt specialist. Dominates on dirt tracks at mile distances.
- **Real Horse**: Dirt racing legend. Won 2010 JBC Classic. Nicknamed "The Dirt King."
- **VA**: Ohwada Hitomi

### Special Week
- **Alias**: Spe-chan (スペちゃん)
- **Personality**: Bright, earnest country girl from Hokkaido. Wants to make both her moms proud by becoming Japan's best Umamusume. Loves her mom's cooking and detailed food reports. Terrible poker player — moves with her emotions. Can tell different milk brands apart by taste.
- **Racing Style**: Versatile middle-distance runner (2400m). Strong finisher with excellent stamina.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1998 Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby) and 1999 Japan Cup. Sired Buena Vista (6 G1 wins) and Cesario. Half-sibling to Silence Suzuka and Grass Wonder (all sired by Sunday Silence).
- **VA**: Waki Azumi
- **Height**: 158cm
- **Teammate**: Team Spica (anime), Team Sirius (game)

### Super Creek
- **Personality**: Warm, maternal figure who treats teammates like her children. Gentle and caring but fiercely protective.
- **Racing Style**: Long-distance stayer (3000m+). Relentless, grinding pace.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1989 Kikuka Sho and 1990 Tenno Sho (Spring).
- **VA**: Yuuki Aoi

### Symboli Rudolf
- **Alias**: The Emperor (皇帝)
- **Personality**: Regal, commanding leader of Tracen Academy. Brilliant strategist who analyzes every race like chess. Natural leader beloved by all.
- **Racing Style**: Perfect all-rounder — no weakness at any distance or surface.
- **Real Horse**: Japan's first undefeated Triple Crown winner (1984). Won 7 G1 races. Greatest Japanese racehorse of the 20th century.
- **VA**: Tadokoro Azusa

## T

### T.M. Opera O
- **Alias**: The Absolute King
- **Personality**: Flamboyant showman who believes he is the greatest horse in the universe — and he might be right. Dramatic, boastful, theatrical. Wears an opera cape. Despite the ego, his skills back it all up.
- **Racing Style**: Unstoppable middle-to-long distance monster. Won 8 consecutive G1 races — an all-time Japanese record.
- **Real Horse**: Won 8 G1 races, 7 in a single season (2000). Considered one of the greatest Japanese racehorses ever.
- **VA**: Tokui Sora
- **Iconic Rivalry**: Meisho Doto (finished 2nd to Opera O six times before finally winning)

### Taiki Shuttle
- **Personality**: Hot-blooded, impulsive American-style runner. Speaks in English phrases. Shonen protagonist energy.
- **Racing Style**: Mile specialist with devastating turn of foot.
- **Real Horse**: First Japanese horse to win a G1 race abroad (1998 Prix Jacques Le Marois in France).
- **VA**: Ohsubo Yuka

### Tamamo Cross
- **Personality**: Cool, calculating strategist. Treats racing like a puzzle. Loves solving complex problems.
- **Racing Style**: Versatile runner. Smart positioning and devastating late kick.
- **Real Horse**: Dominated 1988 with 4 major titles. Famous "Grey Derby" rivalry with Oguri Cap.
- **VA**: Kaida Yuki

### Tokai Teio
- **Alias**: The Miracle Comeback Kid (奇跡の復活)
- **Personality**: Charismatic, flashy, loves being the center of attention. Hides deep determination behind a playful smile. Best friends with Mejiro McQueen.
- **Racing Style**: Explosive middle-distance runner. Career defined by injuries and miraculous comebacks.
- **Real Horse**: Won 1991 Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby). Returned from a career-threatening fracture to win the 1993 Arima Kinen — one of the greatest moments in Japanese sports history.
- **VA**: Machico

### Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi
- **Personality**: Fragile constitution but pure heart. Special Week worries about her health.
- **Racing Style**: Come-from-behind closer.
- **Real Horse**: Upset Special Week in the 1999 Kyoto Daishoten. Member of the Golden Generation.
- **VA**: —

## V

### Vodka
- **Alias**: The Tank (ウオッカ)
- **Personality**: Tomboyish, rebellious, and utterly fearless. Hates being treated differently as a filly. Runs with a chip on her shoulder.
- **Racing Style**: Powerful middle-distance runner who beats colts. Won the Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby) — a race fillies rarely attempt.
- **Real Horse**: First filly in 64 years to win the Tokyo Yushun (2007). Won 7 G1 races. Japan's all-time leading money earner. Beat Daiwa Scarlet by 2cm in the legendary 2008 Tenno Sho (Autumn) photo finish.
- **Iconic Rivalry**: Daiwa Scarlet — roommates and rivals who defined 2007–2008.
- **VA**: Ohashi Ayaka
- **Height**: 165cm (2cm taller than Daiwa Scarlet — the margin of her 2008 Tenno Sho win!)
- **Teammate**: Team Spica (anime)

---

## W

### Winning Ticket
- **Personality**: Energetic, hot-blooded, and always shouting about victory. Brash but fiercely loyal.
- **Racing Style**: Middle-distance runner with explosive acceleration.
- **Real Horse**: Upset Biwa Hayahide to win the 1993 Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby) at 15-1 odds.
- **VA**: Watanabe Keiko

## Racing Terminology Quick Reference

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Leader (逃げ)** | Front-running — leads from the gate, tries to wire the field |
| **Betweener (先行)** | Mid-pack, moves in the stretch |
| **Chaser (差し)** | Comes from behind, saves energy, late kick |
| **Closer (追込)** | Dead last early, monstrous final 400m |
| **G1 / Grade 1** | Highest level of horse racing — championship races |
| **Filly** | Female horse under 4 years old |
| **Colt** | Male horse under 4 years old |
| **Triple Crown** | Winning all three classics: Satsuki Sho, Tokyo Yushun, Kikuka Sho |
| **Tenno Sho** | "Emperor's Prize" — Spring (3200m) and Autumn (2000m) |
| **Arima Kinen** | Year-end G1 Grand Prix (2500m), voted by fans |
| **Kikuka Sho** | Classic G1 for 3-year-olds (3000m), tests stamina |
| **Golden Generation** | Special Week, Seiun Sky, King Halo, El Condor Pasa, Grass Wonder, Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi — the legendary 1997 cohort |
