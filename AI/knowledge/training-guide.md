## What is Career Mode?

Career Mode is the **primary game mode** — you train an Umamusume over ~3 years (78 turns) to build their stats, learn skills, clear race goals, and create a veteran for PvP and future inheritance.

Each completed run produces a **Veteran Umamusume** used in:
- Team Trials (PvP)
- Daily Races
- Future runs as a Legacy (parent for inheritance)
- Room Matches

---

## Career Mode Flow

1. **Select scenario**
2. **Pick trainee** (the Uma you're training)
3. **Pick 2 legacy parents** (for Inspiration/inheritance)
4. **Pick 6 support cards** (5 yours + 1 friend's)
5. **Train for ~78 turns** (Early July Year 1 → Late Dec Year 3)
6. **Complete goal races** to progress
7. **Finish scenario** → Veteran created!

## What You Do Each Turn

### 1. Stat Training (main action)
Pick one of 5 courses. Each course:
- Costs energy
- Gives stats (boosted by assigned support cards)
- May trigger support card events
- Builds friendship with assigned cards

**Don't train with low energy!** Fail chance increases → you can lose stats.

### 2. Rest
- Randomly restores **30, 50, or 70 energy**
- Safe way to recover
- Use when energy is low (below ~40%)

### 3. Recreation (Outing)
- Improves mood by at least 1 stage
- Can restore energy
- If Pal/Friend card is equipped: chain events with big bonuses
- Use when mood is Bad or Awful

### 4. Races
- Optional non-goal races give **stats + SP + fans**
- But they cost energy!
- Only run optional races when:
  - You need more fans for a goal
  - Your energy is high
  - The race is a good fit for your aptitudes

### 5. Infirmary
- Small energy recovery
- Chance to remove negative conditions
- Use when you have a negative condition (e.g., "Headache")

### 6. Skill Shop
- Buy skills anytime (doesn't cost a turn!)
- Spend SP on skills that match your strategy and distance
- Prioritize: recovery skills (endurance) > acceleration > velocity

## Energy Management — The Golden Rule

**Train at high energy, rest at low energy.**

| Energy Level | Action |
|---|---|
| 70–100 | Train! Best results |
| 40–69 | Train cautiously, or Rest if close to a race |
| 20–39 | Rest or Recreation |
| <20 | Rest immediately — training failure = stat LOSS |

## Mood Management

| Mood | Training Effect | How to Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 😊 **Great** | +10% stats | Keep it! |
| 🙂 **Good** | +5% stats | Keep it! |
| 😐 **Normal** | Normal | Consider Recreation |
| 😟 **Bad** | −5% stats | Recreation ASAP |
| 😫 **Awful** | −10% stats | Recreation immediately |

Mood decays naturally over turns. Use Recreation periodically.

## Goal Races

Every trainee has mandatory career goals:
- "Finish 1st in X race by Y date"
- "Reach Z fans by Y date"
- Fail = run ENDS early (use Alarm Clock for retries, max 3 per run)

### Tips for goal races:
1. **Check the race conditions** — track, distance, surface
2. **Match your strategy** — Front Runner won't work well from gate 16
3. **Buy skills BEFORE entering** — can't buy during pre-race phase
4. **Alarm Clocks** are precious — save for critical goals
5. **Fan count matters** — do optional races if you need more fans

## Typical Career Mode Run Strategy

### Year 1 (Junior):
- Focus on **building stats** — train aggressively
- Clear **debut race** early
- Build friendship with support cards
- Aim for Mood: Good+

### Year 2 (Classic):
- **Inspiration event** in Early April — big stat boost!
- Clear mid-tier goals
- Run optional races for fans
- Start buying key skills

### Year 3 (Senior):
- **Final inspiration event** in Early April
- Clear late-stage goals
- Scenario finale
- Go all-out on skills for the final races

## After the Run

Your veteran's final stats are **permanent**. They can't be modified but can be:
- Used in Team Trials and Daily Races
- Used as a Legacy for future runs
- Re-trained from scratch (same trainee, new run = new veteran)

**Pro tip**: Even failed runs produce usable veterans! They still pass Sparks in inheritance.

## Quick Tips for Consistent Success

1. **Balance your deck** — 2 Speed + 2 Stamina + 1 Power + 1 Wit
2. **Level support cards to 30+** — this matters more than anything else
3. **Mood management** — keep it Good or Great
4. **Energy management** — never train below 30%
5. **Buy skills early and often** — they make races easier
6. **Run optional races** when energy is high for extra stats
7. **Stack synergies** — same strategy skills + matching green skills + proper aptitudes
8. **Alarm Clocks = 3 per run max** — save for critical goals only
9. **Check the pre-race screen** — view opponent stats before committing
10. **Friendship training** — max friendship on support cards = biggest bonuses
