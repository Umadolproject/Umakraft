## What is Inspiration (Inheritance)?

Inspiration is how veteran (previously trained) Umamusume pass down stats, aptitudes, and skills to new trainees. Think of it as **breeding** — two "parents" (legacies) pass their traits to the "child" (trainee).

At the start of each Career Mode run, you select:
- **1 trainee** (the Uma you're training now)
- **2 legacies** (veterans from previous runs)
- Each legacy also has their own 2 legacies ("grandparents" — 4 total)

Inspiration triggers at **3 points** during the run:
1. **Start of career** (initial inspiration)
2. **Early April — Classic year** (Year 2)
3. **Early April — Senior year** (Year 3)

## Spark Types

Legacies provide 4 types of Sparks:

| Spark | Color | What It Gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Blue Sparks** | 🔵 | Base stats (Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, Wit) |
| **Pink Sparks** | 🩷 | Aptitude upgrades (Track, Distance, Strategy) |
| **Green Sparks** | 🟢 | Unique skill hints from the legacy |
| **White Sparks** | ⚪ | Skill hints + small stat bonuses from learned skills and G1 wins |

---

## Blue Sparks (Stats)

Blue Sparks give raw stat boosts at the start and during year 2/3 events.

| Blue Spark Stars | Initial Bonus | Inspiration Event Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| 1★ | +5 | +1–10 |
| 2★ | +12 | +1–16 |
| 3★ | +21 | +1–28 |

The stat chosen is random, but **higher stats = more stars**:

| Stat Value | 1★ | 2★ | 3★ |
|---|---|---|---|
| <600 | ~90% | ~10% | 0% |
| 600–1100 | ~50% | ~45% | ~6% |
| 1100+ | ~20% | ~70% | ~10% |

**Key takeaway**: Train your legacies to high stats (1100+) for better Blue Spark quality!

## Pink Sparks (Aptitudes)

Pink Sparks raise Track, Distance, and Strategy aptitudes. Total stars across ALL 6 ancestors (2 parents + 4 grandparents):

| Total Pink Stars | Aptitude Increase |
|---|---|
| 1–3★ | +1 grade (e.g. G → F) |
| 4–6★ | +2 grades (e.g. G → E) |
| 7–9★ | +3 grades (e.g. G → D) |
| 10–18★ | +4 grades (e.g. G → C) |

**Max during initial inspiration**: A rank (S can only be reached in Year 2/3 events)

### Aptitude → Pink Spark Chance:

| Aptitude | Track | Distance | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| G | 10% | 10% | 10% |
| B | 90% | 90% | 85% |
| A | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| S | 105% | 105% | 110% |

**Key takeaway**: Legacies with A or S aptitudes almost always pass Pink Sparks!

## Green Sparks (Unique Skills)

- ★★★ (3-star) veterans automatically get Green Sparks
- ★★ (2-star) or lower cannot get Green Sparks
- Green Sparks give **1–3 hint levels** for the legacy's unique skill
- The trainee can learn the **inherited (weakened) version** of the unique skill

---

## White Sparks (Skill Hints + Bonuses)

White Sparks come from two sources:

### 1. Skills the legacy learned
- Only the **base version** (not upgraded/gold version)
- Learning the gold version increases base spark odds to 40–70%
- If the trainee already knows the skill → small stat bonus instead

### 2. G1 Race victories
Every G1 race the legacy won gives a White Race Spark with specific bonuses:

| G1 Race | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Japanese Derby | Guts + Tokyo Racecourse ○ |
| Arima Kinen | Speed + Nakayama Racecourse ○ |
| Tenno Sho (Spring) | Speed + Spring Runner ○ |
| Tenno Sho (Autumn) | Speed + Fall Runner ○ |
| Japan Cup | Speed + Tokyo Racecourse ○ |
| Satsuki Sho | Power + Nakayama Racecourse ○ |
| Kikuka Sho | Speed + Kyoto Racecourse ○ |

Full list on the wiki — every G1 win = more Sparks!

## How to Build Good Legacies

### For Stat Inheritance:
1. Train legacies to **1100+ in desired stats** (70% chance of 2★ Blue Sparks)
2. Aim for high Speed + Stamina as foundation
3. Complete G1 races for White Race Sparks

### For Aptitude Inheritance:
1. Want Dirt aptitude? Use Dirt-A legacies (100% chance of Pink Sparks for Dirt)
2. Stack 6 ancestors with the same desired aptitude
3. 4 ancestors with A in a track = ~4 grades of boost

### For Skill Inheritance:
1. ★★★ veterans pass unique skills (Green Sparks)
2. Buy useful skills during the legacy's run → they appear as White Sparks
3. Winning G1s = extra White Sparks with useful green skills attached

|---|---|
| More Speed | 1100+ Speed stat |

| Green skills | Parents who won many G1 races |

## Example

**Parent 1**: 1100 Speed, 1000 Stamina, Dirt A  
**Parent 2**: 1100 Stamina, 900 Speed, Triple Crown winner (3 G1s)

→ Child starts with: ~+26 Speed, ~+26 Stamina, 4 Dirt aptitude grades, unique skill hints, plus White Sparks from G1 wins!

The grandparents' stats ALSO count — stack all 6 ancestors for maximum inheritance power.
