## Skill Types

### Green Skills (Condition/Track-Specific)
Activate only under certain conditions. Increase a stat at race start.

| Icon | Stat |
|---|---|
| 🏃 Shoe | Speed |
| ❤️ Heart | Stamina |
| 💪 Arm | Power |
| 🔥 Fire | Guts |
| 📜 Scroll | Wit |

**◎ (double circle)** = large boost (200 SP) · **○ (single circle)** = moderate boost (90 SP)

### Common categories of green skills:
- **Track direction**: Right-Handed, Left-Handed
- **Racecourse**: Tokyo, Nakayama, Hanshin, Kyoto, Chukyo, Sapporo, Hakodate, Fukushima, Niigata, Kokura, Oi, etc.
- **Distance type**: Standard Distance (multiples of 400m), Non-Standard Distance
- **Ground**: Firm Conditions, Wet Conditions (good/soft/heavy)
- **Season**: Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter
- **Weather**: Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, Snowy
- **Post position**: Inner Post (gates 1-3), Outer Post (gates 6-8)
- **Strategy**: Maverick (solo strategy), Competitive Spirit (many same strategy), Target in Sight (favorite same strategy)
- **Popularity**: Long Shot (4th favorite or below)

## Racing Strategies & Key Skills

Skills are divided by the 4 racing strategies (Front Runner, Pace Chaser, Late Surger, End Closer) and 4 distance categories (Sprint, Mile, Medium, Long).

### Front Runner (逃げ) Key Skills
- **Front Runner Straightaways/Corners ◎○** — velocity on straights/corners
- **Dodging Danger** — avoid getting surrounded early-race
- **Leader's Pride** — pass/challenge ability early/mid-race
- **Second Wind** — acceleration when NOT in the lead mid-race
- **Final Push** — keep the lead on final corner
- **Early Lead** — get to the front early

### Pace Chaser (先行) Key Skills
- **Pace Chaser Straightaways/Corners ◎○** — velocity on straights/corners
- **Shrewd Step** — navigate smoothly
- **Hydrate** — recover endurance mid-race
- **Prepared to Pass** — break out of pack on final corner
- **Stamina to Spare** — decrease fatigue approaching mid-race
- **Head-On** — acceleration when at the front late-race

### Late Surger (差し) Key Skills
- **Late Surger Straightaways/Corners ◎○** — velocity on straights/corners
- **Position Pilfer** — increase velocity mid-race
- **Slick Surge** — acceleration late-race
- **Outer Swell** — velocity when passing outside on final corner
- **A Small Breather** — recover endurance late-race
- **Fighter** — acceleration when trying to pass

### End Closer (追込) Key Skills
- **End Closer Straightaways/Corners ◎○** — velocity on straights/corners
- **Standing By** — decrease fatigue mid-race
- **Masterful Gambit** — move up late-race
- **Straightaway Spurt** — acceleration on straight in last spurt
- **Levelheaded** — regain composure when path is jammed
- **Strategist** — widen field of view when at the back late-race

## Distance-Specific Skills

### Sprint (短距離 — ~1200m)
- **Sprint Straightaways/Corners ◎○**
- **Sprinting Gear** — acceleration on a straight
- **Gap Closer** — spurting ability from behind late-race
- **Huge Lead** — maintain lead when far ahead mid-race

### Mile (マイル — ~1600m)
- **Mile Straightaways/Corners ◎○**
- **Shifting Gears** — passing ability from front mid-race
- **Acceleration** — acceleration when passing mid-race
- **Speed Eater** — steal velocity from runners behind

### Medium (中距離 — 2000m–2400m)
- **Medium Straightaways/Corners ◎○**
- **Rosy Outlook** — decrease fatigue from front mid-race
- **Steadfast** — fight back when passed on final corner
- **Thunderbolt Step** — avoid getting surrounded mid-race

### Long (長距離 — 3000m+)
- **Long Straightaways/Corners ◎○**
- **Deep Breaths** — decrease fatigue entering a straight
- **Extra Tank** — regain energy when close to exhaustion
- **Passing Pro** — decrease fatigue when passing

## Unique Skills

Each Uma Musume has a **unique skill** that only she can learn. These are powerful signature skills that define their playstyle.

### Inherited Unique Skills
Unique skills can be **inherited** via the Legacy/Inspiration system during Career Mode. The trainee gets a weakened (継承/inherited) version of the legacy Uma's unique skill.

Key Uma with powerful inheritables:
- **Seiun Sky** — excellent Front Runner unique skill for inheritance
- **Maruzensky** — strong Front Runner unique
- **Oguri Cap** — infamously powerful all-rounder unique

## Skill Point Efficiency Tips

- **Green skills (◎)** at 200 SP / 174 eval = 0.87 ratio — good value per eval point
- **Green skills (○)** at 90 SP / 129 eval = 1.43 ratio — even better per-point value
- **Strategy-specific skills** vary — 0.90–1.81 ratio
- **Balance**: don't overload on green skills at the expense of core racing skills
- **Stack synergies**: combo Corner Adept + Corner Acceleration + Corner Recovery for corner dominance
