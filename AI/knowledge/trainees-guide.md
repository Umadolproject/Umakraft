## How Trainee Cards Work

Each Uma Musume has multiple "alt" versions (different costumes/cards) with unique:
- **Stat growth bonuses** (Speed/Stamina/Power/Guts/Wit %) — totals 30% for ★★★, 20% for ★★, 10% for ★
- **Aptitude ratings** for surface (Turf/Dirt), distance (Sprint/Mile/Middle/Long), and strategy (Front/Pace/Late/End)
- **Unique skills** — each card has its own unique skill

### Rarity & Stat Bonus
| Rarity | Total Stat % | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ★★★ (SSR) | 30% | Main gacha targets |
| ★★ (SR) | 20% | Budget options |
| ★ (R) | 10% | Starter / free characters |

## Stat Growth — What the Numbers Mean

Stat growth bonuses affect how fast each stat rises during Career Mode training:
- **Speed**: Win races, key for all distances
- **Stamina**: Survive longer races (2400m+ needs high stamina)
- **Power**: Acceleration + uphill performance + dirt handling
- **Guts**: Last-spurt endurance + fighting spirit
- **Wit (Intelligence)**: Skill activation rate + positioning

A card with `Speed: 20%, Power: 10%` leans into speed training — great for Sprint/Mile runners.

## How to Read Aptitudes

Each card has aptitude ratings from **G** (worst) to **A** (best) and sometimes **S** for:
- **Surface**: Turf / Dirt
- **Distance**: Sprint (~1200m) / Mile (~1600m) / Middle (~2000–2400m) / Long (~3000m+)
- **Strategy**: Front Runner (逃げ) / Pace Chaser (先行) / Late Surger (差し) / End Closer (追込)

**Tip**: An A in a distance means the Uma performs well there. Check full wiki for exact ratings per card.

## EN vs JP Cards

The English version follows the JP release order (accelerated schedule). Cards marked "JP Only" haven't been released in EN yet. Many ★★★ alts for popular characters (like Daiwa Scarlet's Nuit Étoilée or Vodka's Fiery Aqua Vitae) are JP-only at the time of writing.

## Quick Tips

1. **Stat growth totals 30% for ★★★** — check the distribution to know training focus
2. **Card matters more than character** — the right alt with good growth + skills beats any "strong" character with wrong build
3. **Dirt is rare** — if you need a Dirt runner, pick from the short list above
4. **Inheritance uses ANY card** — even if you don't race the character, their card can pass unique skills via legacy
5. **Check the wiki** for exact aptitudes (G through A) per card — the table above is a summary
