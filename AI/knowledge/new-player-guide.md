## What is Umamusume: Pretty Derby?

A raising + racing simulation game. You play as a Trainer helping Umamusume (horse girls) grow stronger through training to win races. Each Umamusume has personal career goals — completing them makes you a successful Trainer.

## Game Modes

### 1. Career Mode (main mode)
The core training loop. Each run you:
- Pick **1 trainee** (Umamusume), **2 legacies** (for stat/skill inheritance), and **6 support cards** (for stat bonuses, events, and skills)
- Train through weekly turns across different stats
- Clear career race objectives
- Complete the scenario (URA Finale is the launch scenario)

**Key**: A clever arrangement of support cards can make ANY Umamusume a winner.

### 2. Team Trials (PvP)
- Build teams of 15 Umamusume (3 per race type: Sprint, Mile, Medium, Long, Dirt)
- Race against other players' teams
- Classes 1–6 — promoted weekly if above class average
- Higher classes = better rewards

### 3. Daily Races
- 3 races per day for **Monies** or **Support Points**
- 4 difficulty levels: Easy → Normal → Hard → Extra Hard
- Higher difficulty = more currency

### 4. Room Match
- Custom races against friends
- Host, Join, Spectate, or Sign-Up races
- Set your own track and settings

### 5. Story Events
- Limited-time events with exclusive stories and Support Card rewards
- Earn points via Career Mode or Event Missions
- Max rewards at 1,450,000 points
- Story events give Roulette Tokens → Bingo cards → reward cards

## Scouting (Gacha)

- **150 Carats** = 1 scout · **1500 Carats** = 10 scouts
- **200 scouts (30,000 Carats)** = pity/choose a highlighted character or card
- Rates: ☆/R **79%** · ☆☆/SR **18%** · ☆☆☆/SSR **3%**

### Best cards at launch:
- **SSR Super Creek (Piece of Mind)** — recovery skill + extremely long lifespan (~2 years meta)
- **SSR Fine Motion (Wave of Gratitude)** — Wit card, good in every scenario

### Best pick ticket choices (after using free Make Debut ticket first):
- **Maruzensky (Formula R)** — strong Front Runner, great for inheritance
- **Oguri Cap (Starlight Beat)** — strong all-rounder, good Dirt aptitude (rare!)

### Cards to save Carats for:
- **SSR Kitasan Black (Speed)** — the most iconic card, long lifespan, great for Front Runners
- **SSR Rice Shower** — powerful in Aoharu Hai scenario

### Characters to save for:
- **Seiun Sky** — excellent Legacy for Front Runner inheritance
- **Oguri Cap (Ashen Miracle)** — infamously powerful skills for many tracks
- **Mihono Bourbon (CODE: ICING)** — strong Front Runner for Mid/Long, long-lasting PvP performance

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the game free-to-play friendly?
Yes — plenty of F2P experiences. However, top PvP performance requires lots of gacha pulls. You CAN do well without spending money; SR cards are strong and flexible.

### Why can't I use my new scout in races?
You must complete at least ONE Career Mode run with them first (don't need all career goals).

### I keep failing race objectives. Help?
Career Mode RNG is tough early on. As you upgrade support cards, it gets easier. Join the fan Discord for help.

### What's the best Umamusume to start with?
The one you like! But strong meta picks: **Maruzensky** (Front Runner), **Oguri Cap** (all-rounder), both have Dirt aptitude.

### How do I get Dirt-capable Uma?
At launch, only: Haru Urara, El Condor Pasa, Taiki Shuttle, Maruzensky, and Oguri Cap have strong Dirt aptitude.

## Quick Tips for New Trainers

1. **Scout strategically** — save 30,000 Carats for pity (200 pulls)
2. **Level support cards to 30+** — SSR cards matter more than which Uma you pick
3. **Join a club** — monthly rewards, Friend Points, item trading
4. **Do daily races** — consistent Monies and Support Points
5. **Use pick ticket AFTER free Make Debut ticket** — avoid dupes
6. **Plan ahead** — EN version follows JP release order (accelerated schedule)
7. **Balance your deck** — mix Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, Intelligence support cards
8. **Inheritance matters** — good legacies = stronger trainees
